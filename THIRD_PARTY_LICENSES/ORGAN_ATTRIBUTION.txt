Pitea MHS sampleset: Lars Palo, Graham Goode and Panos Ghekas.
Pipe recordings by Lars Palo, released under CC BY-SA 2.5.
https://familjenpalo.se/vpo/pitea-mhs/
https://familjenpalo.se/sites/default/files/sampleset/packages/PiteaMHS20250727.orgue
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.5/

Modified for this project: six-stop grand registration, mono mix for each of
MIDI 60-75, 24.4140625kHz resampling, 3072-sample attacks and phase-matched
sustain loops up to 2048 samples, IMA ADPCM compression and restart metadata.
The derived organ_*.mem sample data are shared under the same CC BY-SA 2.5.
See organ_assets.json for source sample SHA256, registration and gain.
The full license is ORGAN_LICENSE.txt (ORGAN_SAMPLE_LICENSE.txt in the exported
FPGA package). DSP/HDL source is authored for this project.
